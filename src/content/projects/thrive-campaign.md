## College Assignment

## Project Overview

For this college assignment, I created a digital campaign and accompanying campaign website for Thrive, an alcohol-free functional beer brand.
The goal was to introduce two lesser-known beers — Thrive Play and Thrive Unwind IPA — to a new target audience aged 25–45 in Flanders.

My concept positions the two beers as two moods people switch between throughout their day:
#PlayMode (active, social, energetic) and #UnwindMode (relaxed, calm, slow).
The campaign celebrates both — because being productive is exhausting… and so is doing nothing. Fortunately, Thrive made something for both.

## Concept — "Choose Your Flow"

The core idea:
Two products. Two moods. Two ways to Thrive.

Everyone balances fast moments and slow moments — and this campaign invites people to show which one they're in. The hook encourages participation, self-expression, and playful switching between the two modes.

This became the backbone of the campaign visuals, messaging, and interaction design.

## Target Audience

25–45 year olds in Flanders
Health-conscious, socially active, casually sporty, and open to new experiences.
The concept connects to their lifestyle through relatable everyday moments.

## Key Features

### 1. Campaign Website

- Explains the #PlayMode / #UnwindMode concept
- Visual introduction of both beers
- User-generated gallery where people share how they Thrive
- QR integration with offline event
- Fun, bold, colourful UI inspired by the campaign palette
- Microinteractions reflecting the "switching modes" theme

### 2. Social Challenge

"Show Us How You Thrive"
Users upload a photo or video in either #PlayMode or #UnwindMode.
Their content can be featured on Thrive's socials or the campaign site.

### 3. Pop-Up Park Activation

Because the assignment required connecting online and the real world, I designed a simple, low-budget brand activation:
Thrive in the Park.

People can literally switch between modes:

- PlayZone: light, funny mini-activities
- UnwindZone: slower, calming mini-activities
- Free Thrive samples
- Stickers, tote bags, mini-can goodies
- QR flyer linking back to the online challenge

The park becomes a physical metaphor for the campaign concept.

## Visual Identity

Bright, energetic colours contrasted with calm blues and neutrals.
Fun typography and bold layout choices emphasised switching and contrast.
#PlayMode and #UnwindMode each have distinct yet complementary colour profiles.

## Technologies & Tools Used

- Figma — UX, UI, prototyping
- Illustrator & Photoshop — campaign assets, concept visuals
- Brand & audience research frameworks from class

## Process

### Research

- Thrive brand identity
- Trends in alcohol-free beer
- Behaviour and values of the 25–45 demographic
- Digital participation campaigns

### Concept Development

Started from the insight that people constantly shift between activity and rest — just like Thrive's two beers.
Developed the mood-switching metaphor into #PlayMode / #UnwindMode.

### Iterations

- Multiple layouts and visual systems
- Sketches of the park activation flow
- Website prototypes with switching animations

## Reflection

### Strengths:

- Strong, memorable hook
- Clear link between product and lifestyle
- Accessible participation mechanic
- Smooth online–offline connection

### To improve:

- Further refine incentive structure for participation
- Test UI for clearer distinction between modes

## Outcome

A full campaign concept with:

- Pitch presentation
- Visual identity system
- Campaign website prototype
- Social challenge concept
- Offline activation idea
- UGC integration and digital flow
