## Project Overview

Scrapforest is a fictional travel destination where lost objects find a second life—and a community of their own.
This project challenged me to design a homepage that blends playful storytelling, custom illustration work, and contemporary UI design into one coherent experience.

The result is a warm, character-driven website full of humor, texture, and handmade charm, aimed at curious travelers who enjoy unconventional destinations.

## Technologies & Tools

- Figma – layout, structure, and high-fidelity UI
- Photoshop + Illustrator – custom illustrations, textures, and visual elements
- Hand-drawn assets – no templates, no AI-generated graphics
- User-friendly content hierarchy & clear CTAs

## Concept & Visual Language

The concept centers around the idea that every lost item—keys, socks, bottle caps—forms an ecosystem with its own lore.
To bring this world alive, I developed a soft, earthy color palette, paper-like textures, and illustrations with a gentle, storybook feel.
The goal was to surprise visitors the moment the page loads, using a conceptual hero image that immediately communicates:
This is not a typical tourist destination.

The graphic style stays consistent across all sections: curved paths mimicking forest trails, rounded shapes, cozy typography, and warm micro-details that create a sense of place.

## Key Features

### Conceptual Hero Section

A custom illustrated hero featuring Scrapforest's natural landscape mixed with lost objects. Includes an intro explaining what makes the country special and who it's for.

### Meet the Citizens

Introduces the Scrappers—the quirky citizens made from lost items. Cute, humorous character illustrations bring personality and charm.

### Top 3 Things To Do

Visually highlighted featured attractions:

- Litterfall Glade – must-see
- The Noodle Nest – must-eat
- The Fold-Out Retreat – must-stay

Each includes a brief story, pricing, and CTAs.

### Local Delicacies

Two signature dishes of Scrapforest, illustrated and described with whimsical detail.

### Places to Stay

A featured promo stay + secondary option:

- Acorn Haven
- The Bookspine Inn (with a limited-time discount)

Each includes an illustrated rating system, pricing, and booking CTA.

### Reviews

Lighthearted reviews designed with custom illustrated rating icons to reinforce the handmade aesthetic.

### Tourist Office + Footer

Practical information wrapped in a warm, friendly tone, closing the page with consistency and personality.

## Process

1. Developed multiple styleboards to define textures, color direction, and illustration style
2. Created structure sketches and wireframes to plan layout and hierarchy
3. Iterated the homepage design through feedback loops
4. Built a fully custom set of illustrations, icons, backgrounds, and UI components
5. Documented all steps, from idea to final layout
