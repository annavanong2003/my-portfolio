## Project Overview

An expressive mixed-media portrait assignment focused on bold experimentation and creative freedom. The goal was to explore the concept of the face/head using unconventional materials and digital tools. Each portrait was crafted to evoke emotion, identity, and surreal aesthetics through layered techniques and unexpected combinations.

## Technologies & Materials Used

- Traditional media: paper, paint, graffiti, ropes, stones, mirrors
- Digital tools: photography, scanner, Photoshop, Illustrator
- Mixed techniques: collage, texture overlays, color manipulation

## Key Features

- Conceptual portraits focused exclusively on the face or facial elements
- Integration of physical and digital media for hybrid compositions
- Emphasis on surrealism, abstraction, and emotional depth
- Use of unconventional materials to challenge traditional portrait norms
- Created both in class and independently to encourage personal exploration
