---
title: Banana Bread
description: "Moist and flavorful banana bread with a crunchy cinnamon nut topping"
---

## Ingredients

- 190 g self-rising flour
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- A pinch of cloves
- 150 g unsalted butter, softened
- 140 g light brown sugar
- 60 g dark brown sugar
- 2 large or 3 small very ripe bananas
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 2 eggs
- 1 tablespoon cane sugar
- Extra cinnamon
- 60 g roughly chopped nuts

## Instructions

1. Mash the bananas together with the vanilla extract using a fork
2. Cream the butter with the light brown sugar and dark brown sugar
3. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing after each addition
4. Add the mashed bananas and mix again
5. Sift the flour together with the baking powder, cinnamon, and cloves
6. Gently fold the dry ingredients into the batter
7. Butter and flour your baking tin (you can also line it with parchment paper if you like)
8. Pour the batter into the tin
9. Mix the cane sugar, cinnamon, and nuts with a spoon or fork, then sprinkle this mixture on top as a topping
10. Bake in a preheated oven at 170°C for 45–50 minutes
11. Check doneness with a toothpick, then let the cake cool on a wire rack
