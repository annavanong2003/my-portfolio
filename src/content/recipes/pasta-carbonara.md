## Ingredients

- 400g spaghetti
- 200g pancetta or guanciale
- 4 large eggs
- 100g Pecorino Romano cheese, grated
- Black pepper to taste
- Salt for pasta water

## Instructions

1. Cook pasta in salted boiling water until al dente
2. Dice and cook pancetta until crispy
3. Beat eggs with grated cheese and black pepper
4. Reserve pasta water, then drain pasta
5. Mix hot pasta with pancetta
6. Remove from heat and quickly stir in egg mixture
7. Add pasta water to create creamy sauce
8. Serve immediately with extra cheese
