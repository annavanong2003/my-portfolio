---
title: Chocolate Chip Cookies
description: "Recreating the famous Levain bakery Chocolate Chip cookies"
---

## Ingredients

- 230 g cake flour
- 275 g all-purpose flour
- 3 g salt
- 8 g cornstarch
- 6 g baking soda
- 280 g unsalted butter
- 285 g brown sugar
- 115 g granulated white sugar
- 5 eggs (2 whole eggs + 3 egg yolks)
- 165 g walnuts
- 450 g chopped dark chocolate

## Instructions

1. Melt the butter in a saucepan and let it cool.
2. Whisk together both types of flour, the salt, cornstarch, and baking soda in a medium bowl.
3. Combine both sugars in a stand mixer fitted with the whisk attachment and mix on medium speed.
4. Slowly stream in the melted butter until the mixture becomes creamy.
5. Add the 2 whole eggs, then the 3 yolks, one at a time, mixing until each is fully incorporated.
6. (Switch to the paddle attachment.) Mix in the dry ingredients until just combined.
7. Fold in the chopped chocolate and walnuts with a rubber spatula.
8. Transfer the cookie dough to a bowl, cover it, and let it rest in the refrigerator for at least 45 minutes.
9. Portion the dough into 85 g balls and keep them refrigerated until you're ready to bake.
10. Bake at 180°C for 15–17 minutes, or until the edges are golden brown.
