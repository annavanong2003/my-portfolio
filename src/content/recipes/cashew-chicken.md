---
title: Cashew Chicken
description: "A flavorful stir-fry with tender chicken and crunchy cashews"
---

Serves 4–5 people

## Ingredients

- 4 chicken breasts (or thighs), in cubes
- 3 bell peppers, sliced into squares
- White part of 1 spring onion, sliced
- Fresh ginger, sliced
- 30 g (or a generous handful) cashew nuts

For the sauce:

- 2 tablespoons potato starch or cornstarch
- 3 tablespoons light soy sauce
- ½ tablespoon sugar
- 1 tablespoon vinegar or rice vinegar
- 1 cup water

## Instructions

1. Toast the cashew nuts in a dry pan over high heat.
2. Season the chicken cubes with salt and pepper, then add a little neutral oil.
3. Heat a wok over high heat.
4. Stir-fry the chicken in neutral oil until lightly browned.
5. Meanwhile, prepare the sauce by mixing the starch, sugar, soy sauce, vinegar, and water together.
6. Remove the chicken from the wok and stir-fry the bell peppers until just tender.
7. Add the ginger slices and the white part of the spring onion to the peppers.
8. Return the chicken to the wok.
9. Pour in the sauce and mix well. Add a little extra water if the sauce becomes too thick.
10. Serve hot with rice or noodles.

## Tips

- Add extra vegetables like carrots, celery, cucumber, or broccoli for more variety
- Add a pinch of MSG for extra umami flavor
