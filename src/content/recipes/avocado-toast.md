## Ingredients

- 2 slices whole grain bread
- 1 ripe avocado
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Cherry tomatoes
- Red pepper flakes (optional)
- Olive oil

## Instructions

1. Toast bread until golden and crispy
2. Mash avocado with lemon juice, salt, and pepper
3. Spread avocado mixture on toast
4. Top with halved cherry tomatoes
5. Drizzle with olive oil
6. Sprinkle with red pepper flakes if desired
7. Serve immediately
