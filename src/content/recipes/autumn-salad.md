---
title: Autumn Salad with Apple and Goat Cheese
description: "A colorful fall salad with candied nuts, crispy prosciutto, and maple vinaigrette"
---

Serves 5 people

## Ingredients

- 30 g pecans
- 30 g walnuts
- 30 g sunflower seeds
- 3 tablespoons maple syrup
- 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 100 g prosciutto
- 340 g iceberg lettuce, raw
- 200 g arugula
- 1 medium apple
- 63 g goat cheese
- 50 g goji berries
- 77 g pomegranate seeds

Maple Vinaigrette:

- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 3 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- 1 tablespoon mustard
- 1 tablespoon maple syrup
- Fresh thyme
- Fresh sage
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. In a mixing bowl, combine the maple syrup, cayenne pepper, and cinnamon. Toss the pecans, walnuts, and sunflower seeds in the mixture until coated.
3. Spread the nut mixture in a single layer on one side of the baking sheet.
4. Tear the prosciutto into pieces and place them on the other side of the baking sheet.
5. Bake for about 7 minutes, or until the nuts are toasted and the prosciutto is crisp. Keep a close eye on them.
6. Wash the iceberg lettuce and arugula, then place them in a large salad bowl.
7. Remove the baking sheet from the oven and let the nuts and prosciutto cool.
8. Slice the apple into thin half-moon slices and add them to the salad along with the goat cheese.
9. Top the salad with the roasted nuts, crispy prosciutto, goji berries, and pomegranate seeds.

Maple Vinaigrette:

1. Combine the olive oil, apple cider vinegar, mustard, maple syrup, thyme, and sage in a small bowl.
2. Whisk with a fork until smooth. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

## Nutritional Facts

- Nutrition per 100g salad: 140 kcal | Per serving: 309 kcal
- Nutrition per 100g vinaigrette: 442 kcal | Per serving: 88 kcal
- Allergens in salad: nuts, milk, apple
- Allergens in vinaigrette: mustard, apple
